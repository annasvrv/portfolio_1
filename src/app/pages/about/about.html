About me

From the steppes of Ukraine to a small Mediterranean port — the long way to the front end.

I started in GIS — geocoding, spatial data, the kind of pixel-precision work that demands attention to detail. Climbed from junior to mid-level, then leaned into a long interest in interfaces.

Now I’m an HTML/Markup developer at BrainRocket in Larnaca. The Mediterranean shaped how I think about pace and quiet — I try to bring those qualities into my work.
