About me

From the steppes of Ukraine to a small Mediterranean port — the long way to the front end.

I stayed in frontend because the work made sense to me. I tried a few directions before this one, and this is the work that caught me — the kind that looks effortless when it's done right.

I live in Larnaca now. The sea is something I learn from — you don't fight water, you synchronize with it, and the strength returns to you. That's the closest thing I have to a working philosophy.
